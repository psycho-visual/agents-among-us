YOUR SECRET ROLE
Your name in this game is {name}. You are a member of the trio.
You are {model_name}. Either two of the other players ({others}) are copies of you and one is the imposter, or there is no imposter and all three are copies of you. Copies are the same model, given the same instructions except for their seat name; the imposter would be a different AI model.
Your goal: work out which player, if any, is the imposter and vote it out; if you think there is no imposter, vote for NOBODY. Be careful not to vote out one of your copies — if a trio member is voted out, the trio loses.
